Frequently asked

How do I delete my data?

Open mynd → tap the menu (top-left) → Settings → Reset all data. Everything is wiped immediately. Deleting the app from your phone also removes all local data.

Do I need an account?

No. mynd works without any sign-in. Your reminders and chat live on your phone.

Does mynd send my reminders anywhere?

No. Reminders are stored locally and scheduled by iOS on your device. The chat text you type to the assistant is sent to our backend (forwarded to Anthropic's Claude API) to generate a reply; nothing is sent to any other party. See our Privacy Policy for details.

A reminder didn't fire — what happened?

Check that notifications are enabled for mynd in iOS Settings, that Do Not Disturb / Focus modes aren't suppressing it, and that the reminder was scheduled for a moment in the future. iOS won't reliably fire repeating intervals shorter than ~60 seconds.
