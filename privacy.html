Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026 · Operated by Jeremiah Freeman

Overview

mynd is a reminders app. To use it, you create an account. Your reminders themselves stay on your device — we don't keep a copy on our servers. We only store the minimum we need to identify your account and provide the chat feature.

What we collect

  • Your account identifier. When you sign in we create an account record containing a Supabase user ID and the email or identifier returned by your sign-in provider (Apple, Google, or the email you typed).
  • Your name, if you choose to share it during Apple or Google sign-in. This is optional and used only to greet you in the app.
  • Sign-in metadata like the time of your last login, used to keep your session valid.

What we don't collect

  • Your reminders, chat content, and history are saved in your device's local storage and are not uploaded to our servers.
  • No advertising IDs, no analytics SDKs, no third-party trackers are bundled in the app.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for advertising.

What is sent off your device

When you chat with mynd — to describe a reminder, ask a question, or just talk — the text of your message is sent over an encrypted HTTPS connection to our backend, which forwards it to Anthropic's Claude API to generate a response. The reply is shown to you.

  • We do not log the content of these messages on our backend beyond what's needed to forward the request and return the response.
  • Anthropic's handling of API requests is governed by their Usage Policies and Privacy Policy.
  • We send only the message text and the conversation turns we need to produce a useful reply. We do not send your device ID, contacts, photos, calendar, or location.

Sign-in providers

If you sign in with Apple or Google, that provider receives the request to authenticate you. We receive back only:

  • A stable identifier for your account.
  • Your email address (or, for Apple, a hide-my-email proxy address if you choose that option).
  • Your name, if you chose to share it during sign-in.

We use Supabase (supabase.com) as our authentication and identity backend. Supabase processes your account data on our behalf under their terms of service.

Location

If you grant location permission for location-triggered reminders, location is read on your device to evaluate triggers and is not transmitted off-device.

Storage and security

  • Reminders, chats, and preferences live in iOS's app sandbox and benefit from device-level encryption.
  • Account records live in our Supabase database in encrypted form.
  • All network traffic uses HTTPS.

Deleting your data

  • Open Settings → Profile → Delete account inside the app to permanently delete your mynd account and all server-side records associated with it. If you signed in with Apple, this also revokes mynd's access to your Apple ID.
  • Open Settings → Data & Export → Reset all data to wipe all reminders, chats, and preferences from this device immediately.
  • Deleting mynd from your phone removes the on-device data, but does not delete your account on our servers. Use the in-app Delete Account flow for that.
  • To request deletion outside the app, email jaythinkshappiness@gmail.com.

Data retention

We keep account records for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, the record is removed from our database promptly (typically within minutes) and from backups within 30 days.

Children

mynd is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child under 13 created an account, please contact us so we can delete it.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export, or delete your data using the in-app controls or by contacting us at the email below. We will respond within a reasonable time, generally within 30 days.

Changes

If we materially change this policy, the updated text — with a new "Last updated" date — will be posted at this URL. Continued use after a change means you accept the new policy.

Contact
