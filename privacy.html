Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 27, 2026 · Operated by Jeremiah Freeman

Overview

mynd is a reminders app that runs on your device. Your reminders, history, and chat content stay on your iPhone — not on our servers.

What we collect — and don't

  • No account is required. We don't ask for your name, email, phone number, or any identifying information to use the app.
  • Your reminders and chat content are saved in your device's local storage and are never uploaded to our servers.
  • Notifications are scheduled by iOS on your device; we don't send push notifications from a server.
  • No advertising IDs, analytics SDKs, or third-party trackers are bundled in the app.

What is sent off your device

When you chat with mynd — to describe a reminder, ask a question, or just talk — the text of your message is sent over an encrypted HTTPS connection to our backend, which forwards it to Anthropic's Claude API to generate a response. The reply is shown to you.

  • We do not log the content of these messages on our backend beyond what's needed to forward the request and return the response.
  • Anthropic's handling of API requests is governed by their Usage Policies and Privacy Policy.
  • We send only the message text and the conversation turns we need to produce a useful reply. We do not send your device ID, contacts, photos, calendar, or location.

Location

If you grant location permission for location-triggered reminders, location is read on your device to evaluate triggers and is not transmitted off-device. If a future version of mynd adds features that send location anywhere, that update will explain what is sent and to whom, and we'll ask before doing it.

Storage and security

  • Data lives in iOS's app sandbox and benefits from device-level encryption (the same protection iOS gives every app).
  • All network traffic uses HTTPS.
  • Our backend keeps no chat logs and stores no user-identifiable data.

Deleting your data

  • Open Settings → Reset all data to wipe everything from this device immediately.
  • Deleting mynd from your phone removes all local data.
  • Because we don't store data on our servers, there is no remote account to delete.

Children

mynd is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If we materially change this policy, the updated text — with a new "Last updated" date — will be posted at this URL. Continued use after a change means you accept the new policy.
