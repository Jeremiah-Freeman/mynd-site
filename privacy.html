Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026 · Operated by Jeremiah Freeman

Overview

OpenMynd (mynd) is a reminders app you talk to. Your reminders, chats, and history are stored on your device — we don't keep a copy of them on our servers. Some features only work by sending specific pieces of information to a service that does the work (the AI, maps, weather). This page lists every one of them.

What we store on our servers

  • Your account identifier. When you sign in we create an account record containing a Supabase user ID and the email or identifier returned by your sign-in provider (Apple, Google, or the email you typed).
  • Your name, if you choose to share it during Apple or Google sign-in. It is used only to greet you in the app.
  • Sign-in metadata like the time of your last login, used to keep your session valid.
  • Bug reports you choose to send. If you use the in-app bug report, the report includes your note, an optional screenshot, and diagnostic information about the app's recent activity, which can include the names and schedules of your reminders. Nothing is sent unless you send it.

What is sent off your device to do its job

  • The AI (Anthropic). When you talk to mynd — to set a reminder, ask a question, or just talk — your message is sent over HTTPS to our backend, which forwards it to Anthropic's Claude API. To give a useful answer, the request can also include the recent conversation, a summary of your existing reminders, the places you have saved (such as Home or Work), and, if you've allowed location, your approximate location and city. Our backend passes these through and does not keep them. Anthropic's handling of API requests is governed by its Privacy Policy.
  • Speech recognition (Apple). When you hold the button and speak, your voice is transcribed by Apple's speech recognition, which may process audio on Apple's servers under Apple's privacy policy. mynd does not upload your audio anywhere else.
  • Maps and places (Google). When a reminder involves a place ("near a pharmacy", "at Trader Joe's on Hawthorne"), the place name and your approximate location are sent to Google Maps Platform to find matching locations. These searches may pass through our backend, which briefly caches the public search results (business names and addresses) so repeated searches are faster; it does not link them to your account. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Weather (Open-Meteo). If you ask about the weather, your approximate coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo to fetch a forecast.

We do not send your contacts, photos, calendar, or advertising identifier anywhere.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no advertising IDs, no analytics SDKs, no third-party trackers.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information for advertising, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Sign-in providers

If you sign in with Apple or Google, that provider receives the request to authenticate you. We receive back only:

  • A stable identifier for your account.
  • Your email address (or, for Apple, a hide-my-email proxy address if you choose that option).
  • Your name, if you chose to share it during sign-in.

We use Supabase (supabase.com) as our authentication backend. Supabase processes your account data on our behalf under its terms of service.

Location

Location is optional. If you allow it, mynd uses it on your device to trigger location-based reminders (geofences). It is sent off the device only for the purposes listed above — finding places with Google, giving the AI your approximate whereabouts, and fetching weather — and we do not store your location on our servers or keep a location history.

Storage and security

  • Reminders, chats, and preferences live in iOS's app sandbox and benefit from device-level encryption.
  • Account records live in our Supabase database in encrypted form.
  • All network traffic uses HTTPS.

Deleting your data

  • Open Settings → Profile → Delete account inside the app to permanently delete your mynd account and all server-side records associated with it. If you signed in with Apple, this also revokes mynd's access to your Apple ID.
  • Deleting mynd from your phone removes everything stored on the device — your reminders, chats, and preferences.
  • Deleting the app does not delete your account on our servers. Use Delete account in the app for that.
  • To request deletion outside the app, email jaythinkshappiness@gmail.com.

Data retention

We keep account records for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, the record is removed from our database promptly (typically within minutes) and from backups within 30 days. Bug reports you send are kept for up to 60 days and then deleted automatically.

Children

mynd is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child under 13 created an account, please contact us so we can delete it.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export, or delete your data using the in-app controls or by contacting us at the email below. We will respond within a reasonable time, generally within 30 days.
